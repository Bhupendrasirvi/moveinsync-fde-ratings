Where the Commuter Experience Breaks Down

1. App Stability & Verification

False-positive "Not downloaded from Play Store" lockouts and auto-update SQLite migration crash loops prevent users from opening the app.

2. Boarding Friction

Mandatory "Seat Belt Safety First" popups gate OTP visibility while riders stand beside cabs in a rush.

3. Live Tracking & ETAs

GPS telemetry latency and unrealistic ETAs that deviate 3x from real Google Maps traffic.

4. Catalytic Anti-Pattern

Unskippable, modal ride rating dialogs drive frustrated riders directly to the Google Play Store to leave 1-star revenge ratings.
